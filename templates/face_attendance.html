Demo security limitation

This prototype uses OpenCV LBPH face matching. It can make mistakes and does not detect presentation attacks such as printed photos or phone videos. Use it only with informed employee consent, keep manual clock-in available, and do not use it as the sole basis for payroll or disciplinary decisions.

How it works

  1. Allow camera access in your browser.
  2. Face the camera in similar lighting to enrollment.
  3. Click “Start live check” to capture a baseline frame.
  4. Move your face toward the randomly requested side of the video frame, then click “Verify and clock in/out”.
  5. If no shift is open, HUB pulse clocks the recognized employee in. If a shift is open, it clocks that employee out.

Your attendance event is saved in the existing HUB pulse database. The camera frame is processed on this server and is not stored during scanning.
