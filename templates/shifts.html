How late arrivals are calculated

HUB pulse records the number of minutes after the scheduled start. A late flag is shown only when the arrival exceeds the employee's configured grace period. Existing attendance rows are retained; their new late_minutes field defaults to zero because historical scheduled times cannot be reconstructed reliably.
